KIDS & FAMILY SAFE

Privacy Policy for ZunoSpark

Effective Date: October 31, 2026

Summary in Plain English

ZunoSpark does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything stays 100% on your device.

1. Information We Collect

ZunoSpark does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). All gameplay records, explorer profile names, spark counts, and session stats are stored entirely on the user's local device using IndexedDB and never sent to any external server.

2. Third-Party Services & Analytics

ZunoSpark does not use third-party analytics (like Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.), advertising SDKs, or tracking cookies.

3. Advertisements

ZunoSpark is completely ad-free. There are no banner ads, interstitials, rewarded videos, or third-party marketing materials.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Because ZunoSpark is intended for children ages 3–6, privacy is our highest priority:

  • We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • We comply with Google Play Families Policy Requirements.
  • No personal data of children is ever collected or requested.

5. Offline Operation

ZunoSpark is built offline-first. Once installed, it operates without requiring an internet connection.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub project repository.
